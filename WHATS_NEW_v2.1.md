# What’s new in CycleFlow v2.1

*A short note for anyone living with PMDD, ADHD brain-fog, or the sheer exhaustion of tracking a body that doesn’t feel predictable.*

CycleFlow v2.1 is built for **low friction on bad days** and **gentle clarity on better ones** — not another dashboard that judges you.

---

## For the days that hurt

- **Pattern Stream first** — see the shape of recent weeks before you dive into logging. Good days sit higher; heavier stacks sit lower. No spreadsheet required.
- **30-day summary next** — tap a day, jump there, breathe. Context before detail.
- **Collapsible everything** — open only what you need today. The app remembers your layout; tomorrow can look different.
- **FlowDock** — only a bright `›` on the left until you open it (Graph · 30 days 🗓️ · Log · Save Day 📅). **FlowBar** is off by default; turn it on when you’re ready to save.

## For the days you need control

- **FlowBar** (when enabled) — date, symptoms, one tap. Unsaved changes show a small amber hint.
- **Data management** — delete by date range and by type (full days, symptoms only, mood/clarity/fog, notes). **Erase everything** needs typing `DELETE` *and* holding the button for 3 seconds.
- **Toasts, not nagging** — save, copy, import confirmations that disappear on their own.

## After you update the app

If CycleFlow is already on your **Home Screen**, open it once from Safari or pull to refresh so v2.1 loads (PWA service worker).

---

## Still the same promise

- **Private on your device** — export when *you* choose.
- **Free, open source, no paywall** — share with someone who needs it.
- **Not medical advice** — a mirror and a logbook, not a diagnosis.
